About the hotel

A small hotel that pays attention.

Hotel Kabir was built around a simple idea: the details you notice are the ones that make a stay.

Crisp linen and blackout curtains. Water pressure that actually works. A breakfast with fresh parathas and filter coffee, served until late morning because holidays shouldn’t start with an alarm.

Whether you’re here for a wedding in the family, a week of meetings or a few days away, our team takes care of the small things so you don’t have to think about them.
